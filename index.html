PREAMBLE
We, the members of Z WARRIORS, in order to establish a fair, transparent, and democratic community, do hereby ordain and establish this Constitution, dedicating ourselves to justice, freedom of expression, and responsible governance. Explanation: The Preamble is the spritual and functional foundation of Z WARRIORS, designed to ensure fairness, transparency, and democracy in every decision, action, and interaction. Core Principles • Justice: This guarantees the impartial enforcement of all rules for every member equally, including disciplinary measures, recalls, and elections. • Freedom of Expression: Members are allowed to voice opinions, jokes, or criticism as long as it is done civilly and respectfully, without abusive or discriminatory content. Fun and creativity are encouraged within these boundaries. • Responsible Governance: This principle binds administrators, the CJl and the President to adhere to all constitutional provisions while managing the group efficiently and upholding the core values. Significance The Preamble guides all laws and actions and cannot be bypassed. It serves as the ultimate basis for conflict resolution. Ultimately, the Preamble is the soul of Z WARRIORS, ensuring that all laws, elections, debates, and disciplinary actions are aligned with justice, democracy, and respect.
Law 1: The Supreme Judicial Empowerment Act
To preserve justice, fairness, and accountability within the Z Warriors Constitution, the Chief Justice of Z Warriors (CJI) shall be vested with the following powers: Veto Referral Power - The CJl may temporarily block any Presidential or Administrative decision for 24 hours and refer it to a full-group vote, ensuring members have a say before enforcement. Final Trial Authority - The CJI shall preside over all trials or disciplinary hearings within the group. Their judgment shall be binding, except when explicitly overturned by a Presidential Decree. Vote of Justice - The CJI may, once per term, call a "Vote of Justice," where members decide on one major unresolved issue by compulsory voting, overriding all other authorities (including the President). Constitutional Safeguard Power - The CJI may strike down any new law or amendment that contradicts the Basic Structure (Respect, Democracy, Free Expression, Presidential Authority, etc.), unless 3/4 majority of the group supports it. Emergency Oversight Power - During a declared "Group Emergency," the CJl automatically becomes Co-Ruler, with equal administrative authority, though the President retains the final word. Term Security Power - The CJI cannot be removed mid-term except by a 3/4 majority of the group, ensuring independence of judiciary. Judicial Summon Power - The CJI may summon any member, including Administrators, to justify their actions or inactivity before the group. Sole Judicial Adjudication - The CJI shall be the sole presiding Judge for all formal civil and criminal court cases brought before the group's judicial body.
Law 2: The Member Referendum Initiation Act
This provision outlines the process for active members to trigger a Mandatory Referendum on administrative changes that have stalled. The result of this referendum is binding. Trigger: The referendum can be triggered by a petition signed by 10% of active members. Definition of an Active Member: For the purpose of triggering a Mandatory Referendum or any other constitutional provision requiring a membership count, an Active Member is defined as follows: An individual who has sent a minimum of 25 civil and non-administrative messages within the group's main chat during the last 30 calendar days prior to the submission of the petition. Calculation: The 10% is calculated based on the number of active members at the moment the petition is submitted. Limitation: To prevent perpetual gridlock, a new referendum petition can only be initiated after the previous one is resolved or after a period of 1.5 months has passed. CJI Review: The Chief Justice of the I-IANS (CJI) must review the validity of the petition within 24 hours of submission. Referendum Trigger: Once the petition is verified and confirmed to meet the 10% threshold, the CJI must trigger the referendum process within the next 48 hours. Appeals: Any disputes regarding the validity or counting of signatures on the petition may be appealed directly to the President. The President's decision on the petition's validity is final. Simultaneous Petitions: If multiple valid petitions are submitted at the same time, they will be prioritized by their timestamp (first in, first out). Tie Resolution: In the event of a tie during the referendum vote, the President shall cast the tie-breaking vote, which is final.
Law 3: The Party & Alliance Act
To establish a clear hierarchy of appointment and opposition within the Z Warriors governance structure, the following mandates regarding Party Leaders and appointments shall be enforced: Prerogative of the Victor: The Leader of the Political Party securing the majority victory in an election shall hold the exclusive right to appoint the Chief Justice (CJI) and the Elected Administrator. The Winning Party Leader reserves the right to appoint themselves to either of these positions. Rights of the Minority: The Leader of the Party securing the second-highest number of votes (the Minority/Losing Party) shall hold the exclusive right to appoint the Leader of the Opposition (LoP) only if they secure at least 10% of the members' votes, ensuring a balanced check on power. Recall & Reappointment: Party Leaders retain the authority to remove or replace their appointees at any time during the term, provided that such changes do not violate the Basic Structure of the Constitution or leave a critical office vacant for an extended period.
Law 4: Judicial Appointment and Integrity
This provision ensures the impartiality and incorruptibility of the Judiciary by establishing a non-elective appointment process, shielding the Chief Justice (CJI) from political campaign influences. Presidential Consensus: The appointment of a CJI is predicated on the unanimous agreement of the Presidential branch. A candidate may only assume the bench if all serving Presidents formally ratify the selection. Unilateral Executive Appointment: In the event that the Clan is governed by a single President, that individual retains the sole authority to appoint either one or two CJIs, depending on the administrative needs and their personal discretion. Bicameral Executive Appointments: Under a tiered leadership system involving Senior Presidents (SP) and Junior Presidents (JP), the appointments are distributed as follows: The Office of the Senior President is empowered to appoint one (1) Chief Justice. The Office of the Junior President is empowered to appoint one (1) Chief Justice. Discretionary Waiver: Either office may voluntarily choose to waive their right to an appointment, leaving the position vacant or deferring to the other office's selection. Non-Elective Tenure: To prevent corruption and bias, the CJI shall never be chosen through a general election or popular vote, ensuring the role remains strictly merit-based and appointed by the High Command.
Law 5: The Administrator Emergency Power Act
This provision grants Administrators limited, immediate authority to handle minor disruptions, ensuring the group's smooth operation without constant oversight. Declaration: An Administrator may declare a "Micro-Emergency" to immediately enforce minor rules without seeking prior Presidential or CJI approval. Scope of Minor Rules: This power is strictly limited to issues such as spam control or muting a single, temporarily disruptive member. Duration Limit: A Micro-Emergency cannot exceed 6 hours. Any extension beyond this limit requires explicit approval from either the CJI or the President. Mandatory Reporting: The Administrator must report the declaration of the Micro-Emergency and the action taken to the CJI within 30 minutes of its initiation. Logging: The Administrator must formally log the action taken and the duration. Violation: Any violation of the 6-hour limit or failure to report correctly will be escalated to the CJI for disciplinary review. Edge Case: If multiple Administrators declare separate emergencies, each action must be sequentially logged and reported by the respective Administrator. Affected Member Appeal: Any member affected by an action taken under a Micro-Emergency has the right to appeal the action directly to the CJI. The CJI's decision regarding the appeal is final.
Law 6: The Oath Re-Affirmation Act
This provision mandates that all elected officials must publicly reaffirm their Oath of Office mid-way through their term to ensure continued commitment to the group's foundational principles. Requirement: All elected officials (specifically the CJI, VP, LOP & Administrators) must reaffirm their original Oath of Office. Format: The Oath must be reaffirmed via a voice message or by signing an Oath sent in the group chat. Timing: The reaffirmation must occur at the mid-term mark (0.75 months) of their elected tenure. Tracking: Administrators are responsible for tracking and logging the timely submission of the voice messages. Missed Deadline: If an official misses the deadline, the President must issue a formal Notice of Non-Compliance. Delay: The reaffirmation may only be delayed under a special exception granted by the President. Unavailability: In an edge case where an official is physically unavailable, a pre-recorded voice message of the Oath will suffice. Appeal Process: An official who receives a Notice of Non-Compliance may appeal the notice directly to the CJI. Review Timeline: The CJI must review the appeal and issue a binding decision within 12 hours.
Law 7: The Transparency of Vote Act
This provision ensures full transparency and accountability in all group-wide voting processes by requiring the President to make key data public. Mandatory Disclosure: The President must declare the total number of eligible voters and the final turnout percentage for every group-wide vote conducted. Privacy: Only individual vote selections remain private; the aggregate totals and turnout figures are public information. Hiding Turnout: The President is not allowed to hide the mandatory turnout data. Verification: Administrators verify the tally after the count is complete. Audit: If a discrepancy is suspected by any party, the CJI may audit the voting results for accuracy. Challenge: Members may challenge the results within a 24-hour window. Resolution: The CJI resolves any challenge to the voting results with a binding decision. Tie-Breaker: In the event of a tie, the President handles the tie-breaker by casting the final vote.
Law 8: The Member Grievance Petition Act
This provision establishes a formal mechanism for any member to address concerns by submitting a grievance directly to the CJI, ensuring a channel for accountability and resolution. Submission: Any member may submit a formal grievance to the CJI. Definition of Grievance: Grievances qualify if they pertain to rule violations, administrative actions, or abusive behavior within the group. Review Timeline: The CJI must review the submitted grievance within 48 hours of its reception. Hearing Decision: Based on the review, the CJI will decide whether the grievance is valid enough to warrant a formal hearing. Multiple Grievances: Members are permitted to file multiple grievances, with each submission being evaluated separately on its own merits. Enforcement: If the CJI finds the petition valid, they will formally call a hearing to address the issue. Frivolous Cases: Frivolous petitions (those lacking substance or filed in bad faith) may be dismissed with a formal warning to the submitting member. Finality of Decision: The CJI's final decision on the matter is generally binding. Appeal/Intervention: The President may only intervene in the case of a proven procedural error during the CJI's review or hearing process, but not to overturn the substantive ruling itself.
Law 9: The Constitutional Guardrail Act
This provision establishes the Basic Structure of Z WARRIORS, defining the core principles that are permanently safeguarded and cannot be amended by any authority or majority. Core Components: The Basic Structure is defined by the principles of Respect, Democracy, Free Expression, and the established Presidential/CJI authority. Amendment Immunity: This structure cannot be amended under any circumstances. Even a 3/4 majority vote is irrelevant and cannot override this provision. Constitutional Guardrail: The CJI (Chief Justice of the I-IANS) is responsible for immediately blocking any proposal deemed unconstitutional because it conflicts with the Basic Structure. Support: The President must support the CJI in enforcing this essential guardrail when such a conflict arises. Automatic Invalidation: Any new rule or amendment that is found to conflict with the Basic Structure is automatically invalid from its inception. Appeal Status: Decisions based on the protection of the Basic Structure are not subject to appeal as this law is considered absolute and foundational.
Law 10: The Term Limit Extension Act
This provision enforces term limits to promote fresh leadership and prevent the entrenchment of power by restricting the number of consecutive terms an Administrator or the CJI may serve. Consecutive Limit: An Administrator or the CJI (Chief Justice of the I-IANS) cannot serve more than two consecutive terms in the same office. Cooling-Off Requirement: After serving two consecutive terms, the official must observe a mandatory 1.5 months cooling-off period before being eligible to run for that same office again. Partial Term Counting: Only terms of full 1.5 months duration are counted toward the two-term limit. Shorter, partial terms do not trigger the cooling-off period. Role Switching: A member can switch roles (e.g., from Admin to CJI or vice-versa), but the two-consecutive-term limit still applies specifically to the office they are currently holding. Enforcement: The President and the Administration are responsible for tracking these term limits, and any ineligible candidates must be blocked from running. Early Termination: If an official's term ends early (e.g., due to resignation or recall), the term counting for a new office (if they switch roles) is reset to zero. Eligibility Disputes: The CJI may be called upon to review and resolve any disputes over candidate eligibility based on these term limit rules.
Law 11: The Meme Regulation Act
This provision governs the posting of memes and similar informal content to ensure group chat remains manageable and productive by limiting the frequency of posts by individual members. Content Limit: Members are restricted to posting a maximum of 3 memes or similar content items per 10-minute interval. Format Inclusion: Any format counts toward the limit, including memes posted in voice messages or images. Individual Tracking: Each member’s posts are tracked individually; simultaneous posting by multiple members is still monitored against each person's limit. Celebration Exemption: Content posted during official group celebrations or festivals does not count towards the limit, provided the content remains respectful. These special exemptions are announced by an Administrator in advance. Formal Attachments: Memes attached to formal messages are valid as long as the member's individual 3-per-10-minute limit is not exceeded. First Violation: The member receives a warning message from an Administrator. Second Violation: The member is muted for 1 hour. Third Violation: The case is escalated to the CJI (Chief Justice of I-IANS) for review and potential further action. Right to Appeal: A member who has been punished (muted or referred to CJI) can appeal the decision to the CJI within 12 hours. CJI Review: The CJI may reduce or nullify the punishment if a justification (e.g., technical error, misinterpretation) is successfully presented.
Law 12: The Weekly Debate Act
This provision mandates the holding of weekly debates on relevant topics, fostering a space for discussion, engagement, and the free exchange of ideas among members. Frequency: The group will hold weekly debates on topics deemed relevant to the group or wider interests. Participation: Participation is optional; members are strongly encouraged, but not mandated, to join the discussions. Scheduling and Topic: Administrators are responsible for posting the debate topic and the official schedule. Conduct Rule: Personal attacks are strictly forbidden. Violations of the group's respectful conduct rules (abuse, insults, etc.) will be enforced immediately. Controversial Topics: If a debate topic is particularly controversial, the CJI (Chief Justice of I-IANS) may step in to moderate the discussion to ensure fairness and adherence to decorum. Enforcement Steps: Violations of decorum are first met with a warning from an Administrator, then escalated to the CJI for further disciplinary action upon repetition. Written Opinions: Members who miss the scheduled debate are permitted to submit written opinions on the topic. Tie Resolution: If a debate involves voting for a "winner" and results in a tie, the tie is resolved by consensus among the Administrators present. Appeals: Members may appeal any disciplinary actions arising from violations during the debate process directly to the CJI.
Law 13: The Member of the Month Recognition Act
This provision establishes a process for publicly recognizing one member each month for their outstanding positive contributions to the group, promoting encouragement and positive engagement. Frequency: One member per month is recognized for their efforts. Selection Process: The selection is made primarily by the Administrators, who also gather input from the general membership. Self-Nomination: Members can nominate themselves for the recognition. Consecutive Limit: To ensure fair rotation and opportunity for all, a member cannot be recognized consecutively (i.e., in two or more months in a row). Enforcement and Reward: An Administrator announces the monthly winner. The recognition includes a specific reward, such as a special role or an emoji badge, displayed for the month. Co-Recognition: In an edge case where multiple members have equal, outstanding contributions, a co-recognition (recognizing more than one member) may occur. Special Exceptions: Special exceptions related to festivals or group events may allow for temporary, additional recognition outside of the monthly winner rule. Disputes/Appeals: If a member feels they were unfairly overlooked despite significant contribution, they may submit a dispute to the CJI (Chief Justice of I-IANS) for review.
Law 14: The No Ghosting Act
This provision ensures critical group communications are addressed promptly by requiring members to respond to important messages within a defined timeframe to maintain efficient group functioning. Important Messages Defined: Important messages include, but are not limited to: Administrator announcements, official group polls/votes, and CJI (Chief Justice of I-IANS) requests. Justified Absence: Absence from the group, and therefore failure to respond, can be justified by notifying an Administrator in advance. First Enforcement Step: Initial failure to respond triggers a reminder sent by an Administrator. Repeated Failure: If the reminder is ignored repeatedly, the member may face a temporary restriction from group activities. Consolidated Response: If multiple important messages are sent simultaneously (an edge case), a single, consolidated response addressing all points will suffice. Appeals: The CJI is responsible for reviewing any disputes or appeals regarding the validity of a member's justification for non-response or the application of temporary restrictions.
Law 15: The Festival Celebration Act
This provision establishes a framework for organizing official celebrations for festivals or events, designed to encourage group engagement and create a positive environment. Law 16: The Fair Play in Elections Act
This provision mandates that all elections within the group must adhere to fair and transparent procedures, with oversight provided by both the Administration and the CJI to ensure the integrity of the democratic process. Mandate: All elections must follow established fair and transparent procedures. Monitoring: The entire process is monitored by the Administration (Admins) and the CJI (Chief Justice of I-IANS). Administration Duties: Admins are responsible for announcing the election schedule, securely collecting votes, and verifying candidate and voter eligibility. CJI Oversight: The CJI audits the election results and procedures if any dispute arises. This provision encourages members to actively contribute to the group's collective growth by sharing beneficial knowledge, tips, or resources in a positive and constructive manner. Law 21: Presidential Impeachment and Resignation
This provision establishes the framework for ensuring the President remains accountable by outlining a clear process for their removal (impeachment) and the role of the judiciary and general membership. Judicial Removal Basis: The President may be initially removed if the Court of Z WARRIORS finds them guilty of the charges brought against them. Challenging the Verdict: If either party (the President or the accuser) disagrees with the Court's (Judge's) decision, they have the right to post a poll in the group. Impeachment by Vote (Verdict Challenge): If a verdict is challenged via a poll, the President will be removed from their position if ⅗ (three-fifths) of the voters agree to the removal. Removal Without a Case: The President can be removed without a formal court case only if a significantly higher threshold of ⅘ (four-fifths) of the voters agree to the removal in a group-wide poll. Impact of Challenge: Challenging the initial judicial verdict does not fully protect the President; instead, the case outcome then depends entirely on the majority decision of the group membership. Purpose: This dual system guarantees that the highest office remains accountable while simultaneously safeguarding it against the misuse of power or politically motivated judicial decisions.
Law 22: Date Determination and Tenure Negotiation
This provision governs the process for both resignation and impeachment, ensuring these leadership transitions are not chaotic but are instead managed through a structured process overseen by the judiciary to guarantee stability. Oversight Authority: The entire process of resignation and impeachment is overseen by the CJI Bench (Court of Z WARRIORS). Resignation Date Authority: The CJI Bench holds the sole authority to determine the specific date of a President's resignation. This safeguard prevents confusion and ensures smooth transitions. Presidential Influence: The President may request a specific date for their resignation, but the final decision rests exclusively with the Bench. Negotiation Power (Extensions): The Bench has the power to negotiate extensions of the President’s tenure if valid reasons or urgent circumstances (e.g., delayed elections, critical ongoing discussions) require continuity of leadership. Accelerated Date: Conversely, if the President’s continued presence is actively disrupting group harmony, the Bench has the authority to bring the transition date forward. Constitutional Philosophy: This negotiation power reflects the flexibility of the Constitution, balancing strict enforcement of accountability with the wisdom of judicial discretion to protect the integrity of the transition without disrupting group stability. Purpose: This law demonstrates that governance within Z WARRIORS is both principled and pragmatic. This provision serves as a crucial safeguard to prevent the destabilization of leadership, ensuring that impeachment remains a serious tool rooted in justice, not merely political dissatisfaction. Impeachment Failure Threshold: If a group vote for the President's removal is held, the impeachment automatically fails if 60% (⅗) of the members do not support the removal. Immediate Reinstatement: Upon the failure of the impeachment attempt, the President remains in full office with all their powers restored. Frivolous Prevention: This threshold prevents frivolous or repetitive attempts to destabilize the leadership while respecting the collective will of the majority. Subsequent Attempts: Another impeachment attempt can be made, but only if a new, valid court case with new evidence or charges is formally brought forward. Temporary Strengthening: The failure of an impeachment attempt temporarily strengthens the President's position, as it demonstrates current majority support until further disputes arise. Filtering Mechanism: The fundamental requirement of a valid court case acts as a filter, stopping constant impeachment motions and ensuring the process is a serious tool, not a casual instrument of politics. Purpose: This provision shields the group from instability while reaffirming that leadership changes must be rooted in justice and accountability, not mere dissatisfaction. Automatic Role Assumption: Immediately upon resignation or removal (impeachment), the former President will automatically assume the position of Nominated Administrator. Purpose of the Role: This transitional role acknowledges their past contributions and ensures their institutional knowledge and experience continue to benefit the group. Duration: The status of Nominated Administrator lasts only until fresh elections are successfully held for both the CJI and the permanent Administrator posts. Power Level: The power of the Nominated Administrator is limited, serving mainly in an advisory and transitional capacity, rather than a full executive role. Legacy and Respect: This safeguard prevents the sudden exclusion of leaders and promotes respect for their legacy, even when they step down under pressure. Function: The Nominated Administrator acts as a transitional figure, offering guidance and continuity during electoral shifts. Principle: This law reinforces the principle that leadership in Z WARRIORS is service-oriented and dignified, not disposable. Re-nomination Pathway: Once the Nominated Administrator's tenure concludes, they may be re-nominated for a continued role or set aside, depending on a joint decision. Decision Authority for Re-nomination: The choice to re-nominate or set aside the former President is made by the CJI (Chief Justice of I-IANS) and the newly elected President working together. Synchronization Mandate: Elections for the new President shall always coincide with elections for the CJI and Administrator. Purpose of Synchronization: Linking elections ensures a holistic leadership cycle, preventing gaps in power and reaffirming that leadership transitions are a group-wide event, not isolated changes. Return to Presidency: A removed President can return to the office of President, but only if they are allowed to run in the Presidential Election by the Judge and subsequently win in the fresh elections. Underlying Principle: This mechanism preserves fairness while keeping democratic choice at the center, highlighting that no position is permanent and every leader remains accountable to the group’s collective will. Retention of Authority: The President retains full authority to exercise all their powers until the specific resignation date, which is determined by the CJI Bench. Purpose: This prevents confusion and ensures that leadership duties do not stall before the official handover occurs. Power Loss Trigger: The President does not lose their power once impeachment proceedings start; power is lost only after the resignation or removal officially takes effect. Responsibility Clause: All retained powers must be exercised responsibly, with the full awareness that the office is currently in transition. Misuse Intervention: If the President attempts to misuse their power during this transition period, a new case can be filed, and the CJI Bench may immediately intervene to mitigate the damage or accelerate the transition. Rationale: Continued authority is allowed to maintain order and avoid leaderless governance during the period between the decision to transition and the final handover. Core Goal: This rule ensures stability and continuity during times of executive transition.
Tie Resolution Protocol
Voting Entitlement: Both the President and the Chief Justice (CJ) are fully entitled to vote in all electoral or judicial polls conducted within Z WARRIORS. Vote Weight: The votes cast by the President and the CJ count equally with the vote of any other general member. Tie-Breaker Exclusion: Neither the President nor the CJ is designated as a tie-breaker in any voting scenario. Applicability: This rule applies without exception to all elections, judicial rulings (where applicable), and general group polls. Tie Resolution: In the event of a tie in any vote, the result shall remain officially undecided until a re-vote is successfully conducted. Purpose: The protocol ensures the President and CJ can participate fully in decisions while simultaneously maintaining neutrality and fairness when a vote results in a tied situation.
Presidential Removal Clauses

The President can only be removed through a formal No-Confidence Motion passed by at least 80% (⅘) of the total group members. Any member absent or not voting during the motion shall automatically be counted as supporting the President. Once a No-Confidence Motion fails, no new motion against the President can be brought for the next three months. Any attempt to remove the President without following this procedure shall be invalid and unconstitutional. This law is final and binding on all members, including the Chief Justice and Administrators. Any removal of the President that occurs outside the explicit clauses listed below is invalid. The following are the exclusive judicial cases under which a President can be removed: Binding Clause: This entire law is final and binding on all members, including the CJ and Admins. Any presidential removal outside of these five listed clauses is invalid. Application: Applies only if the President knowingly breaks major constitutional laws. Prerequisites: Requires 3 written warnings issued by the Chief Justice (CJ) and solid proof (such as screenshots, messages, or witnesses). Removal Threshold: Removal is valid only if ⅗ (three-fifths) of all members agree via a vote. Loophole Blocked: No verbal or fake proof is allowed; all evidence must be real and verified. Application: Applies if the President blocks, removes, or interferes with the CJ’s duties or the Court’s proceedings. Initial Action: The CJ may temporarily suspend the President, but final removal still requires ⅔ group approval. Loophole Blocked: The CJ cannot act alone; they must present the group with written reasons and verifiable proof. Application: Applies if the President bribes, threatens, or rigs votes or court outcomes. Proof Requirement: Proof must come from at least two independent members (witnesses or evidence sources). The requirement for 3/5 of 16 was: 9.6 → rounded to 10. The President achieved exactly that number. Thus: He was acquited completely. The charges were declared invalid. The case was dissolved. Judicial credibility was shaken. This moment would become symbolic — the survival of executive legitimacy through democratic strength. XX. THE RESTORATION OF THE JUDICIARY Recognizing the danger of a judiciary-free administration, Vardan reinstated Mirza after the disqualification period. Why? This restoration marked the beginning of a slow reconciliation. XXI.
